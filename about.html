About Captain A1

Disciplined security services from a veteran-led team.

Based in Ajmer, Captain A1 Security Services protects people, premises, events, and operations with trained personnel and practical supervision.

Our story

Founded on discipline, reliability, and field readiness.

Captain A1 Security Services was founded by Capt. D.D. Sharma, a veteran with 30 years of military experience. The company brings that discipline into private security, investigation, site protection, and facility support.

Current service coverage includes Ajmer, Kota, Jaipur, and Bharatpur, with expansion planning for Mathura and Agra. Each deployment is shaped around site needs, shift timing, visitor flow, and escalation paths.
