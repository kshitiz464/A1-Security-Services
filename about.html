About Captain A1

A disciplined team for sites that cannot afford uncertainty.

Professional security, protection, manpower, and facility support shaped around each client’s operating reality.

Our story

Experience in uniform, adapted for private security.

Captain A1 Security Services was built around a simple standard: disciplined personnel should also be courteous, observant, and practical at the site where they serve.

Our leadership brings more than three decades of defence and field experience. That background informs how we brief teams, assign responsibility, respond to incidents, and communicate with clients.

We currently support assignments in Ajmer, Jaipur, Kota, and Bharatpur, with additional locations planned as operational capacity expands.
