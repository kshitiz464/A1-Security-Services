About Captain A1 Security Services

Security built on trust. Delivered with discipline.

Captain A1 is a veteran-led security services and manpower solutions company providing trained and verified personnel for communities, businesses, industries, events, and personal protection across Rajasthan.

Our story

Built on experience. Driven by responsibility.

Security is not just about having people on-site. It is about having the right people, the right standards, and the right support behind them.

Veteral led, Captain A1 brings a disciplined and experienced approach to security manpower and workforce solutions. We understand each site before selecting personnel, preparing them for the role, and supporting them throughout the assignment.

From residential communities and commercial spaces to industrial sites, events, and personal protection, our focus stays simple: reliable people, clear standards, and responsible service.
