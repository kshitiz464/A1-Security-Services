Common questions

Useful answers before deployment.

How is security service pricing decided?

Pricing depends on location, duty hours, personnel profile, number of shifts, supervision, equipment, and assignment duration. We provide a requirement-based quotation after the first discussion.

Can you provide temporary or event security?

Yes. Permanent, short-term, event-based, day-shift, and night-shift requirements can be discussed subject to personnel availability.

Do you provide armed guards and personal protection?

Gunmen, PSOs, and bodyguards can be discussed for suitable assignments, subject to lawful licensing, documentation, role requirements, and availability.

Which locations do you currently serve?

Our current focus includes Ajmer and Jaipur, with selected assignments elsewhere in Rajasthan based on scale and supervision needs.
